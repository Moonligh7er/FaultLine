CTConnecticut

8 counties · no county government

CT abolished county governments in 1960; counties remain as geographic areas only. Municipal government is the sole layer for outreach. In 2022 CT designated 9 Councils of Government (COGs) for federal statistical purposes — the COGs matter for regional grant work.

  • Fairfield
  • Hartford
  • Litchfield
  • Middlesex
  • New Haven
  • New London
  • Tolland
  • Windham

Priority targets

  • New Haven (New Haven Co.) — already seeded via SeeClickFix; university partnerships possible.
  • Hartford (Hartford Co.) — state capital; council packet exposure.
  • Stamford / Danbury / Waterbury — Gateway cities; SeeClickFix backends in DB.
  • Bridgeport (Fairfield Co.) — largest CT city by population; not yet seeded.

RIRhode Island

5 counties · no county government

Like CT, RI has no county government — counties are geographic only. Statute dataset RI v0.1.0 is drafted, pending review (clerk-of-record delivery is the audit point). 39 municipalities total; small state means comprehensive coverage is achievable in a single quarter of outreach.

  • Bristol
  • Kent
  • Newport
  • Providence
  • Washington

Priority targets

  • Providence — capital + largest city; proprietary 311 (PVD 311), separate track.
  • Warwick / Cranston — largest suburbs of Providence; sizable DPW operations.
  • Pawtucket / East Providence — dense, older infrastructure; high report density likely.
  • Newport (Newport Co.) — tourism-driven; visible infrastructure has outsized political salience.
