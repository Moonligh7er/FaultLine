Protocol 01 / The Problem

$786 billion in deferred maintenance.

The American Society of Civil Engineers gives US infrastructure a C- grade. You see it every day. Here's what your city hopes you won't do about it.

01

You hit a pothole

Average vehicle repair cost: $306. AAA reports Americans spend $3 billion annually on pothole damage. Your city knows. They're counting on you not filing a claim.

02

You call 311

You wait on hold. You describe the location. You hang up. Nothing happens for weeks. You call again. They have no record of your first call. You give up. They're counting on that too.

03

Nothing changes

Individual complaints are easy to ignore. There's no public record, no accountability, no consequences. The same potholes reappear every spring. The cycle repeats.

Protocol 02 / The Procedure

Three reports.
Real consequences.

We turned individual frustration into collective power, automated the paperwork, and made inaction legally expensive.

Step · 01
📸

Report in 10 seconds

Snap a photo. GPS drops your pin. AI identifies issue and severity. Quick mode skips the paperwork. Voice mode works hands-free while driving.

Step · 02
✅

Community verifies

When 3 people report the same issue, it's community-verified. Our witness network asks nearby users to confirm — often within minutes.

Step · 03
📨

Authority notified

At 10 reports, the app generates a professional demand letter — GPS evidence, photos, fiscal impact projections, statutory citation — and prepares it addressed to the right department. You review and send it in one tap.

Step · 04
⏱️

Clock starts running

A public timer tracks the response. If the deadline passes, the app drafts a formal follow-up notice citing state statute for you to send. The whole community sees who acts and who doesn't.

Protocol 03 / Accountability

Tools that make inaction expensive.

This isn't just a reporting app. It's an accountability platform.

⚖️

Legal demand letter templates

The app generates a formal notice for you to send, citing your state's specific defective-highway statute (MA, RI, NH). Tracks the statutory deadline. You review, sign, and send.

🛡️

Insurance claim packager

One tap generates a timestamped evidence bundle: GPS proof, photos, notification history, community verification. Everything your insurance company needs.

💰

Fiscal impact math

“Fix this pothole for $75 today, or face $12,000 in liability next year.” The letters show authorities the math. Inaction becomes the expensive option.

📊

Infrastructure grades

Every city that clears the data threshold gets a public A–F grade. Response times, fix rates, severity trends — all transparent. Methodology →

🗳️

Election district overlay

See which council districts have the most unresolved issues. Know who represents you. Know their record. Vote accordingly.

🏗️

Contractor accountability

Track which repair contractors do quality work and which ones cut corners. When the same pothole opens three months later, the record shows who signed off.

🎥

Video testimonials

Optional 15-second video reporters can attach to their own escalations. Numbers are ignorable. A face saying “this hazard is on my kid's walk to school” is not.

🔮

Predictive analytics

Designed to flag where damage will likely occur before it happens — seasonal patterns, decay curves, historical data. Intended for pre-deployment reports as pilots roll out.

Fault Line is a self-help documentation tool, not a law firm. Letters and claim packets are generated for you to review and send yourself. This is not legal advice. For a live claim against a municipality, consult a licensed attorney in your state.
